# Types and territories

Explore several approaches before settling on a construction:

| Approach | Useful for | Tradeoff to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Descriptive | Fast category understanding | Can overlap competitors and limit distinctiveness |
| Experiential | Suggesting the user's experience | Familiar metaphors may be crowded |
| Evocative | Suggesting a position or feeling | Needs a clear rationale and context |
| Invented | A distinctive sound or flexible vessel | Can need spelling support and meaning-building |

These are creative tradeoffs, not predictions of legal clearance. Real words and coinages both need investigation. Coinages are not automatically available or culturally neutral.

Develop four different territories tied to the brief. Include an unexpected but relevant option. Generate from distant analogy (how another field does the same job), human experience (what changes for the user), inversion (what the product removes), and collisions between otherwise unrelated fields. No companion skill is required.

For each territory record its association, why it fits the brief, target-market questions and construction ideas. Keep an unusual option long enough to judge it fairly. If it changes the product rather than its name, flag that as a positioning question instead of silently changing scope.
